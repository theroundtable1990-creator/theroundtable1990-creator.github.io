The Roundtable Publisher - Privacy Policy

The Roundtable Publisher is a private tool used only by the owner of The Roundtable accounts to upload its own original videos to its own TikTok account.

Data we access: the TikTok access token granted by the account owner and basic account information (open id, display name) needed to upload videos. We do not access, collect or store data of any other TikTok user.

Storage: the token is stored on a private server, is never shared with third parties and is used only to upload the owner's videos. The owner can revoke access at any time from TikTok settings (Security > Manage app permissions).

No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

Contact: The Roundtable HUB on Telegram.
